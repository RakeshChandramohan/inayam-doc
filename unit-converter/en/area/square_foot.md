## Square Foot (sq ft) Measurement Guide & Area Converter

### Definition
The **square foot** (symbol: **sq ft** or $\text{ft}^2$), plural **square feet**, is an imperial and US customary unit of surface area equal to the area of a square with sides measuring **1 foot** ($12 \text{ inches}$ or $0.3048 \text{ meters}$).

One square foot equals exactly **144 square inches** ($12 \times 12$) or **0.09290304 square meters**. Square feet are universally used in real estate property listings, architecture, interior design, flooring installation, and land survey sizing across North America and the UK.

### How to Calculate Square Foot Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between square feet and metric or imperial area units:

- **Square Feet to Square Meters:** $\text{sq m} = \text{sq ft} \times 0.09290304$
- **Square Feet to Acres:** $\text{Acres} = \text{sq ft} / 43,560$
- **Square Feet to Square Yards:** $\text{sq yd} = \text{sq ft} / 9$
- **Square Meters to Square Feet:** $\text{sq ft} = \text{sq m} / 0.09290304$

#### Calculation Example:
If an apartment floor plan measures **850 square feet**, convert the area to square meters and square yards:

1. **Square Meters Conversion:**  
   $$850 \text{ sq ft} \times 0.092903 \text{ sq m/sq ft} \approx 78.97 \text{ square meters}$$

2. **Square Yards Conversion:**  
   $$850 \text{ sq ft} / 9 = 94.44 \text{ square yards}$$

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many square meters (sq m) are in 1 square foot (sq ft)?
One square foot equals exactly **0.09290304 square meters** ($\approx 0.093 \text{ m}^2$).

#### 2. How many square feet are in 1 square meter?
One square meter equals approximately **10.7639 square feet**.

#### 3. How many square feet are in 1 acre?
There are exactly **43,560 square feet** in 1 acre.

#### 4. How many square inches are in 1 square foot?
There are exactly **144 square inches** ($12 \text{ in} \times 12 \text{ in}$) in 1 square foot.

#### 5. How many square feet are in 1 square yard?
There are exactly **9 square feet** ($3 \text{ ft} \times 3 \text{ ft}$) in 1 square yard.

#### 6. How do I calculate the square footage of a room?
Multiply the room length in feet by the width in feet: $\text{Area (sq ft)} = \text{Length (ft)} \times \text{Width (ft)}$.

#### 7. How do I convert square feet to square meters mentally?
Divide the square footage by 10.7 (or multiply by 0.093). For example, $1,000 \text{ sq ft} / 10.76 \approx 92.9 \text{ sq m}$.

#### 8. How many square feet are in 1 hectare?
One hectare ($10,000 \text{ m}^2$) equals approximately **107,639 square feet** (2.471 acres).
