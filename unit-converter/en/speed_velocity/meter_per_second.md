## Meter per Second (m/s) Speed Guide & Velocity Converter

### Definition
**Meters per second** (symbol: **m/s** or **$\text{m}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$**) is the official SI derived unit of speed and velocity, defined as the distance in meters traveled over a duration of one second.

It is the universal base unit used across physics, aerodynamics, engineering mechanics, scientific research, and orbital mechanics.

### How to Calculate Speed Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between $\text{m/s}$ and other velocity units:

- **m/s to Kilometers per Hour (km/h):** $\text{km/h} = \text{m/s} \times 3.6$
- **m/s to Miles per Hour (mph):** $\text{mph} = \text{m/s} \times 2.23693629$
- **m/s to Feet per Second (ft/s):** $\text{ft/s} = \text{m/s} \times 3.2808399$
- **km/h to m/s:** $\text{m/s} = \text{km/h} / 3.6$

#### Calculation Example:
If Usain Bolt sprinted at a peak speed of **12.4 m/s**, convert his speed to km/h and mph:

1. **Kilometers per Hour Conversion:**  
   $$12.4 \text{ m/s} \times 3.6 = 44.64 \text{ km/h}$$

2. **Miles per Hour Conversion:**  
   $$12.4 \text{ m/s} \times 2.23694 \approx 27.74 \text{ mph}$$

### Key Metric Conversion Ratio: The 3.6 Factor
To convert $\text{m/s}$ directly to $\text{km/h}$, multiply by **3.6**:

$$\text{Speed (km/h)} = \text{Speed (m/s)} \times 3.6$$

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How do I convert meters per second (m/s) to km/h?
Multiply the speed in m/s by **3.6**. For example, $10 \text{ m/s} \times 3.6 = 36 \text{ km/h}$.

#### 2. How many miles per hour (mph) are in 1 m/s?
One meter per second equals approximately **2.23694 mph**.

#### 3. What is the speed of sound in m/s?
The speed of sound in dry air at $20^\circ\text{C}$ (Mach 1) is approximately **343 m/s** (1,234.8 km/h or 767 mph).

#### 4. What is the speed of light in m/s?
The speed of light in a vacuum ($c$) is defined as exactly **299,792,458 m/s** ($\approx 3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$).

#### 5. How many feet per second (ft/s) make 1 m/s?
One meter per second equals approximately **3.28084 feet per second**.

#### 6. What is 100 km/h in m/s?
100 km/h divided by 3.6 equals approximately **27.78 m/s**.

#### 7. How do I convert m/s to knots?
Multiply speed in m/s by 1.94384. For example, $10 \text{ m/s} \times 1.94384 \approx 19.44 \text{ knots}$.

#### 8. How do I convert km/h to m/s mentally?
Divide the speed in km/h by 3.6. For example, $72 \text{ km/h} / 3.6 = 20 \text{ m/s}$.
