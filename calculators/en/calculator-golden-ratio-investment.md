## Golden Ratio Investment Calculator – Calculate Fibonacci Portfolio Allocation

### What is the Golden Ratio in Investing?
The **Golden Ratio** ($\phi \approx 1.618$), derived from the famous **Fibonacci Sequence**, is a mathematical proportion widely observed in nature, architecture, and financial market technical analysis. 

In portfolio construction, investors apply the Golden Ratio to systematically allocate capital between growth (high-risk) and capital preservation (low-risk) asset classes. Using a **Golden Ratio Investment Calculator** provides an objective, mathematical strategy to balance portfolio risk without emotional bias during market volatility.

---

### Mathematical Golden Ratio Asset Allocation Formula

The Golden Ratio portfolio strategy splits total investment capital ($C$) into two harmonious proportions derived from $\phi$:

1. **Low-Risk / Core Assets Allocation ($61.8\%$):**
   $$A_{\text{Low Risk}} = C \times (\phi - 1) = C \times 0.618$$

2. **High-Risk / Growth Assets Allocation ($38.2\%$):**
   $$A_{\text{High Risk}} = C \times \left(1 - 0.618\right) = C \times 0.382$$

Where:
- $\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.6180339887$
- **$C$** = Total capital available to invest

#### Step-by-Step Numerical Example:
Suppose you have a total capital of **$10,000** to construct a balanced portfolio:

1. **Low-Risk Asset Allocation (Bonds, Gold, Fixed Income):**  
   $$\$10,000 \times 0.618 = \$6,180$$

2. **High-Risk Asset Allocation (Equities, Emerging Markets, Crypto):**  
   $$\$10,000 \times 0.382 = \$3,820$$

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. How does the Golden Ratio apply to portfolio rebalancing?
When stock market rallies cause your high-risk allocation to exceed 38.2%, the Golden Ratio strategy prompts you to harvest profits and rebalance capital back into low-risk assets to maintain equilibrium.

#### 2. Why is the 61.8% / 38.2% split popular in technical analysis?
The key Fibonacci retracement levels (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) reflect market psychology and support/resistance zones where prices frequently bounce.

#### 3. Is the Golden Ratio suitable for short-term trading?
The Golden Ratio allocation strategy works best for medium-to-long-term investors seeking disciplined risk management across market cycles.
