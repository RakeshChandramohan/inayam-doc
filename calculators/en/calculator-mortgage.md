## Mortgage Calculator – Calculate Monthly Home Loan Payments & Interest

### What is a Mortgage Calculator?
A **Mortgage Calculator** is an essential financial utility designed to estimate monthly home loan payments, interest charges, and long-term loan amortization. By evaluating loan principal, interest rates, and loan terms (e.g., 15-year or 30-year mortgages), prospective homebuyers can determine overall housing affordability before applying for a home loan.

---

### Formula to Calculate Monthly Mortgage Payment

The standard mathematical formula used by banks to compute fixed monthly mortgage payments ($M$) is:

$$
M = P \times \frac{r(1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}
$$

Where:
- **M** = Monthly mortgage payment
- **P** = Principal home loan amount (Purchase price minus down payment)
- **r** = Monthly interest rate ($\text{Annual Interest Rate} / (12 \times 100)$)
- **n** = Total number of monthly payments ($\text{Tenure in Years} \times 12$)

#### Step-by-Step Numerical Example:
Suppose you borrow a **$300,000** mortgage at an annual fixed interest rate of **4.5%** for a **30-year term (360 months)**:
1. **P** = 300,000
2. **r** = $\frac{4.5}{12 \times 100} = 0.00375$
3. **n** = $30 \times 12 = 360$

Plugging these values into the formula:

$$
M = 300,000 \times \frac{0.00375(1 + 0.00375)^{360}}{(1 + 0.00375)^{360} - 1} \approx \$1,520.06
$$

- **Total Amount Paid Over 30 Years:** $\$1,520.06 \times 360 = \$547,221.60$
- **Total Interest Paid:** $\$547,221.60 - \$300,000 = \$247,221.60$

| Component | Description | Included in Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Interest (I)** | Lender charge for borrowing money | Yes |

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. What is the difference between a 15-year and a 30-year mortgage?
A 15-year mortgage requires higher monthly payments but incurs substantially less total interest over the life of the loan. A 30-year mortgage offers lower monthly payments but costs more in total interest.

#### 2. What is Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)?
PMI is insurance required by lenders when a buyer puts down less than 20% down payment on a conventional home loan.

#### 3. How does a larger down payment affect monthly mortgage payments?
A larger down payment reduces the principal loan balance ($P$), directly lowering your monthly mortgage payment and eliminating PMI requirements.

#### 4. What is mortgage refinancing?
Refinancing replaces an existing home loan with a new loan, typically to secure a lower interest rate, shorten the loan tenure, or cash out home equity.
