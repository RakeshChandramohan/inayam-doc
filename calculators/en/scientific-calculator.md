## Online Scientific Calculator – Trigonometry, Logarithms, and Exponents

### What is a Scientific Calculator?
A **Scientific Calculator** is an advanced mathematical utility capable of calculating complex mathematical, scientific, engineering, and statistical functions beyond basic arithmetic.

Unlike standard basic calculators, an online **Scientific Calculator** handles trigonometric functions ($\sin, \cos, \tan$), inverse trigonometry ($\arcsin, \arccos, \arctan$), natural logarithms ($\ln$), common logarithms ($\log_{10}$), exponents ($x^y$), roots ($\sqrt{x}, \sqrt[n]{x}$), factorials ($n!$), and mathematical constants like $\pi$ (Pi) and $e$ (Euler's number).

### Radians vs. Degrees Mode

- **Degree Mode ($^\circ$):** Measures angles where a full circle equals $360^\circ$. Used in navigation, engineering, and geometry.
- **Radian Mode ($\text{rad}$):** Measures angles where a full circle equals $2\pi \text{ radians}$. Used in calculus, physics, and advanced trigonometry.
  
$$\text{Radians} = \text{Degrees} \times \frac{\pi}{180^\circ}$$

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. What is the difference between $\ln$ and $\log$?
- $\ln(x)$ is the natural logarithm with base $e \approx 2.71828$.
- $\log(x)$ or $\log_{10}(x)$ is the common logarithm with base 10.

#### 2. What is Euler's number ($e$)?
Euler's number ($e \approx 2.718281828459$) is a fundamental mathematical constant that forms the natural base of logarithmic and exponential growth functions.
