## Fixed Deposit (FD) Calculator – Calculate Maturity Interest & Returns

### What is a Fixed Deposit (FD)?
A **Fixed Deposit (FD)** is a low-risk financial savings instrument offered by commercial banks, post offices, and NBFCs. Investors deposit a single lump sum for a fixed tenure (ranging from 7 days to 10 years) at a guaranteed interest rate. Interest can be compounded quarterly or paid out periodically.

Using an online **FD Calculator** helps depositors estimate exact maturity values, interest earnings, and compare bank interest rates instantly.

### Formula to Calculate FD Maturity Amount

The mathematical formula used to calculate compound interest maturity amount ($M$) on a Fixed Deposit is:

$$
M = P \times \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{n \cdot t}
$$

Where:
- **M** = Final Maturity Amount
- **P** = Principal deposit amount
- **r** = Annual interest rate (in decimal format, e.g., $6.5\% = 0.065$)
- **n** = Compounding frequency per year (Quarterly = 4, Monthly = 12, Half-Yearly = 2, Yearly = 1)
- **t** = Total tenure in years

#### Step-by-Step Numerical Example:
Suppose you deposit **₹100,000** at an annual interest rate of **7.0%** compounded quarterly for a tenure of **3 years**:
1. **P** = 100,000
2. **r** = 0.07
3. **n** = 4 (Quarterly compounding)
4. **t** = 3 years ($n \cdot t = 12$)

Plugging these values into the formula:

$$
M = 100,000 \times \left(1 + \frac{0.07}{4}\right)^{12} = 100,000 \times (1.0175)^{12} \approx ₹123,144
$$

- **Total Principal Deposited:** ₹100,000
- **Total Interest Earned:** ₹23,144

### Cumulative vs. Non-Cumulative Fixed Deposits

| Feature | Cumulative FD | Non-Cumulative FD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Interest Payout** | Reinvested until maturity | Paid monthly, quarterly, or annually |
| **Maturity Value** | Higher (due to compounding) | Equals original principal |
| **Best Suited For** | Long-term wealth creation | Retirees needing regular monthly income |

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. How is FD interest calculated?
FD interest is calculated using quarterly, monthly, or annual compound interest formulas based on principal amount, tenure, and bank interest rates.

#### 2. Is interest earned on Fixed Deposits taxable?
Yes. Interest earned on FDs is taxable under income tax guidelines. Banks deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) if total annual interest exceeds statutory thresholds.

#### 3. Are Fixed Deposits safe?
Yes. FDs with commercial banks are backed by government deposit insurance (e.g., DICGC insurance up to ₹5 Lakhs per depositor per bank in India).

#### 4. Can I withdraw my FD before maturity?
Yes, premature withdrawal is permitted by most banks, though it typically incurs a minor penalty fee (0.5%–1.0%) and a slightly lower applicable interest rate.
