## Kilogram (kg) Measurement Guide & Mass Unit Converter

### Definition
The **kilogram** (symbol: **kg**) is the fundamental base unit of mass in the International System of Units (SI). It is used globally across trade, science, engineering, health, and commercial shipping.

Since 2019, the General Conference on Weights and Measures (CGPM) officially redefined the kilogram by fixing the numerical value of the **Planck constant** ($h = 6.62607015 \times 10^{-34} \text{ J}\cdot\text{s}$), replacing the historical physical platinum-iridium prototype (*Le Grand K*).

### How to Calculate Kilogram Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between kilograms and other mass units:

- **Kilograms to Pounds:** $\text{Pounds (lbs)} = \text{kg} \times 2.20462262$
- **Kilograms to Grams:** $\text{Grams (g)} = \text{kg} \times 1,000$
- **Kilograms to Metric Tonnes:** $\text{Tonnes (t)} = \text{kg} / 1,000$
- **Kilograms to Ounces:** $\text{Ounces (oz)} = \text{kg} \times 35.2739619$

#### Calculation Example:
If a shipping container has a net weight of **75 kilograms**, convert the weight to pounds and ounces:

1. **Pounds Conversion:**  
   $$75 \text{ kg} \times 2.20462 \text{ lbs/kg} \approx 165.35 \text{ lbs}$$

2. **Grams Conversion:**  
   $$75 \text{ kg} \times 1,000 \text{ g/kg} = 75,000 \text{ grams}$$

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many pounds are in 1 kilogram?
One kilogram equals approximately 2.20462 pounds (lbs).

#### 2. How many grams are in 1 kilogram?
One kilogram equals exactly 1,000 grams.

#### 3. How do I convert kilograms to pounds mentally?
Multiply the number of kilograms by 2.2. For example, $10 \text{ kg} \times 2.2 = 22 \text{ lbs}$.

#### 4. How many kilograms are in 1 metric tonne?
One metric tonne ($t$) contains exactly 1,000 kilograms.

#### 5. What is the difference between mass and weight?
Mass is the fundamental quantity of matter in an object (measured in kilograms), whereas weight is the gravitational force acting on that mass ($W = m \cdot g$).

#### 6. Why was the definition of the kilogram changed in 2019?
The definition was changed to base the kilogram on a fundamental constant of physics (Planck's constant) rather than a physical metal prototype susceptible to mass drift.

#### 7. How many ounces make 1 kilogram?
One kilogram equals approximately 35.274 avoirdupois ounces.

#### 8. How do I convert kilograms to stones?
Divide the weight in kilograms by 6.35029. For example, $70 \text{ kg} / 6.35029 \approx 11.02 \text{ stones}$.
