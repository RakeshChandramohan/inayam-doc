## Pound per Square Inch (PSI) Measurement Guide & Pressure Converter

### Definition
**Pounds per square inch** (symbol: **psi** or **$\text{lbf/in}^2$**) is an imperial and US customary unit of pressure equal to the force of **one pound-force applied over an area of one square inch**.

One psi equals exactly **6,894.75729 pascals** ($\approx 6.895 \text{ kPa}$ or $0.06895 \text{ bar}$). PSI is the universal standard for vehicle tire pressure inflation, scuba diving tank ratings, air compressor specifications, hydraulic systems, and industrial boiler pressure vessel safety.

### How to Calculate PSI Pressure Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between PSI and metric pressure units:

- **PSI to Bar:** $\text{bar} = \text{psi} \times 0.06894757$
- **PSI to Kilopascals (kPa):** $\text{kPa} = \text{psi} \times 6.894757$
- **PSI to Pascals (Pa):** $\text{Pa} = \text{psi} \times 6,894.757$
- **Bar to PSI:** $\text{psi} = \text{bar} / 0.06894757 \quad (\text{bar} \times 14.50377)$

#### Calculation Example:
If a car owner's manual recommends a front tire pressure of **35 psi**, convert the tire inflation pressure to bar and kilopascals:

1. **Bar Conversion:**  
   $$35 \text{ psi} \times 0.068948 \text{ bar/psi} \approx 2.413 \text{ bar}$$

2. **Kilopascals Conversion:**  
   $$35 \text{ psi} \times 6.89476 \text{ kPa/psi} \approx 241.32 \text{ kPa}$$

### Gauge Pressure (PSIG) vs. Absolute Pressure (PSIA)
- **PSIG (PSI Gauge):** Measures pressure relative to atmospheric ambient air ($0 \text{ psig} = 1 \text{ atm} = 14.7 \text{ psia}$).
- **PSIA (PSI Absolute):** Measures pressure relative to a perfect vacuum ($\text{psia} = \text{psig} + 14.696$).  
*Tire pressure gauges report PSIG values.*

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many bar equal 1 psi?
One psi equals approximately **0.068948 bar** ($1 \text{ bar} = 14.5038 \text{ psi}$).

#### 2. How many kilopascals (kPa) equal 1 psi?
One psi equals approximately **6.89476 kilopascals**.

#### 3. What is standard atmospheric pressure in PSI?
Standard sea-level atmospheric pressure equals **14.696 psi** ($\approx 14.7 \text{ psi}$).

#### 4. What is the recommended PSI for standard passenger car tires?
Most passenger car tires require between **30 psi and 35 psi** ($2.07 - 2.41 \text{ bar}$) cold inflation pressure.

#### 5. What is the difference between PSIG and PSIA?
- **PSIG:** Gauge pressure measuring pressure above atmospheric ambient air.
- **PSIA:** Absolute pressure measuring total pressure above absolute vacuum ($\text{PSIA} = \text{PSIG} + 14.7$).

#### 6. What is a scuba diving tank pressure in PSI?
A fully charged standard aluminum scuba tank holds **3,000 psi** of compressed air ($207 \text{ bar}$).

#### 7. How do I convert PSI to Bar mentally?
Divide PSI by 14.5 (or divide by 15 for a quick estimate). For example, $30 \text{ psi} / 15 = 2.0 \text{ bar}$ (exact is $2.07 \text{ bar}$).

#### 8. How many pascals (Pa) equal 1 psi?
One psi equals exactly **6,894.757 pascals**.
