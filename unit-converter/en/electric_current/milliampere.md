## Milliampere (mA) Current Guide & Electrical Converter

### Definition
The **milliampere** (symbol: **mA**) is a metric unit of electric current equal to **$\frac{1}{1,000}$ of an ampere** ($0.001 \text{ A}$ or $10^{-3} \text{ A}$).

Milliamperes are used universally across microelectronics, smartphone battery capacity ratings (milliampere-hours / **mAh**), medical ECG cardiac monitoring, LED lighting current draw, and low-power sensor circuits.

### How to Calculate Milliampere Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between milliamperes and amperes or battery capacity (mAh):

- **Milliamperes to Amperes:** $\text{Amperes} = \text{mA} / 1,000$
- **Amperes to Milliamperes:** $\text{mA} = \text{Amperes} \times 1,000$
- **Battery Runtime Formula:** $\text{Runtime (hours)} = \frac{\text{Battery Capacity (mAh)}}{\text{Device Load Current (mA)}}$

#### Calculation Example:
If a smartphone has a **5,000 mAh** battery capacity and the phone consumes a steady average current of **250 mA**, calculate the estimated battery operating runtime in hours:

$$\text{Runtime} = \frac{5,000 \text{ mAh}}{250 \text{ mA}} = 20 \text{ hours}$$

### Human Electrical Safety Thresholds in mA
- **1 mA:** Threshold of human electrical perception (faint tingling sensation).
- **5 mA:** Maximum safe current threshold for ground fault circuit interrupters (GFCI).
- **10 mA – 20 mA:** "Let-go" threshold where sustained muscle contraction occurs.
- **100 mA:** Ventricular fibrillation threshold (potentially fatal cardiac arrest).

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many milliamperes (mA) are in 1 ampere (A)?
There are exactly **1,000 milliamperes** in 1 ampere.

#### 2. How do I convert milliamperes to amperes?
Divide the value in mA by 1,000. For example, $500 \text{ mA} / 1,000 = 0.5 \text{ A}$.

#### 3. What does mAh mean on a battery specification?
**mAh (milliampere-hour)** measures total electric charge storage capacity. A 4,000 mAh battery can deliver $4,000 \text{ mA}$ for 1 hour, or $400 \text{ mA}$ for 10 hours.

#### 4. What is the current draw of a standard indicator LED in mA?
A standard electronic indicator LED draws approximately **15 mA to 20 mA** at 2V–3.3V DC.

#### 5. How many mA does a USB 2.0 port supply?
A standard USB 2.0 port supplies up to **500 mA** ($0.5 \text{ A}$) at 5 Volts ($2.5 \text{ Watts}$).

#### 6. What current level in mA is dangerous to humans?
Currents as low as **10 mA to 20 mA** cause painful muscle contraction, while currents above **100 mA** ($0.1 \text{ A}$) through the heart can cause fatal ventricular fibrillation.

#### 7. What is a GFCI safety trip current in mA?
Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter (GFCI) safety outlets trip and disconnect power within milliseconds if a leak current of **4 mA to 6 mA** is detected.

#### 8. How do I convert amperes to milliamperes mentally?
Multiply by 1,000. For example, $2.5 \text{ A} \times 1,000 = 2,500 \text{ mA}$.
