## Horsepower (hp) Power Measurement Guide & Power Converter

### Definition
**Horsepower** (symbol: **hp**) is an imperial unit of mechanical power equal to **550 foot-pounds per second**, approximately **745.69987 watts** ($0.7457 \text{ kW}$), or **33,000 foot-pounds per minute**.

Horsepower is used universally across the automotive industry, marine propulsion, aviation piston engines, industrial electric motor ratings, and lawn equipment engine specifications.

### How to Calculate Horsepower Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between mechanical horsepower and metric power units:

- **Horsepower to Kilowatts:** $\text{kW} = \text{hp} \times 0.74569987$
- **Horsepower to Watts:** $\text{Watts} = \text{hp} \times 745.69987$
- **Horsepower to Metric Horsepower (PS):** $\text{PS} = \text{hp} \times 1.01386967$
- **Kilowatts to Horsepower:** $\text{hp} = \text{kW} \times 1.34102209$

#### Calculation Example:
If an automobile engine produces **250 mechanical horsepower**, convert the engine output to kilowatts:

$$\text{Power (kW)} = 250 \text{ hp} \times 0.7457 \text{ kW/hp} = 186.425 \text{ kW}$$

### Mechanical Horsepower (hp) vs. Metric Horsepower (PS / CV)
- **Mechanical Imperial Horsepower ($\text{hp}$):** $550 \text{ ft-lb/s} = 745.700 \text{ W}$ (US/UK automotive standard).
- **Metric Horsepower ($\text{PS}$ or $\text{CV}$):** $75 \text{ kgf-m/s} = 735.499 \text{ W}$ (German/European automotive standard).  
*1 Mechanical Horsepower equals approximately **1.014 Metric Horsepower (PS)**.*

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many watts equal 1 mechanical horsepower (hp)?
One mechanical horsepower equals approximately **745.7 watts** ($0.7457 \text{ kW}$).

#### 2. How many kilowatts (kW) equal 1 horsepower?
One mechanical horsepower equals approximately **0.7457 kilowatts**.

#### 3. How do I convert kilowatts (kW) to horsepower (hp)?
Multiply kilowatts by 1.341022. For example, $100 \text{ kW} \times 1.341 = 134.1 \text{ hp}$.

#### 4. What is the difference between Mechanical Horsepower (hp) and Metric Horsepower (PS)?
Mechanical hp equals **745.7 W**, whereas German Metric Horsepower (Pferdestärke / PS) equals **735.5 W** ($1 \text{ hp} = 1.014 \text{ PS}$).

#### 5. How did James Watt define Horsepower?
James Watt estimated that a draft pit pony could turn a mill wheel 2.4 times per minute, lifting $33,000 \text{ foot-pounds}$ of work per minute ($550 \text{ ft-lb/sec}$).

#### 6. What is the peak horsepower output of an actual horse?
While James Watt defined continuous working horsepower at 1 hp, a real horse can produce peak bursts of up to **14.9 horsepower** for short durations.

#### 7. What is Brake Horsepower (bhp)?
Brake Horsepower (bhp) measures engine output power measured directly at the engine flywheel before driveline friction losses.

#### 8. How do I convert horsepower to watts mentally?
Multiply horsepower by 750. For example, $10 \text{ hp} \times 750 = 7,500 \text{ watts}$ ($7.5 \text{ kW}$).
