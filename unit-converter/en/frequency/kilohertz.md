## Kilohertz (kHz) Frequency Guide & Frequency Converter

### Definition
The **kilohertz** (symbol: **kHz**) is a metric unit of frequency equal to **1,000 hertz** ($10^3 \text{ Hz}$ or $1,000 \text{ cycles per second}$).

Kilohertz is used universally to measure human audible acoustic sound frequencies ($20\text{Hz} - 20\text{kHz}$), digital audio sampling rates ($44.1\text{kHz} / 48\text{kHz}$), AM radio broadcast carrier bands ($530\text{kHz} - 1700\text{kHz}$), and ultrasonic transducer sonar signals.

### How to Calculate Kilohertz Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between kilohertz and hertz or megahertz:

- **Kilohertz to Hertz:** $\text{Hz} = \text{kHz} \times 1,000$
- **Kilohertz to Megahertz:** $\text{MHz} = \text{kHz} / 1,000$
- **Kilohertz to Gigahertz:** $\text{GHz} = \text{kHz} / 1,000,000$
- **Period Time ($T$):** $T (\text{ms}) = \frac{1}{f (\text{kHz})}$

#### Calculation Example:
If a digital audio recording workstation operates at a **96 kHz** sampling rate, calculate the acoustic cycle period per sample in microseconds:

$$\text{Sample Interval (μs)} = \frac{1}{96 \text{ kHz}} = 0.010416 \text{ ms} \approx 10.42 \text{ microseconds}$$

### Nyquist-Shannon Sampling Theorem Benchmark
In digital signal processing, the Nyquist theorem dictates that to record frequencies up to $20 \text{ kHz}$ (human hearing limit), the digital sampling rate must be at least double ($20 \text{ kHz} \times 2 = 40 \text{ kHz}$), explaining why **44.1 kHz** was chosen for CD audio.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many hertz are in 1 kilohertz (kHz)?
There are exactly 1,000 hertz in 1 kilohertz.

#### 2. How many kilohertz equal 1 megahertz (MHz)?
There are exactly 1,000 kilohertz in 1 megahertz ($1,000,000 \text{ Hz}$).

#### 3. What is the frequency range of human hearing in kHz?
The human ear detects acoustic sound frequencies from **0.02 kHz (20 Hz) to 20 kHz (20,000 Hz)**.

#### 4. Why is CD audio sampled at 44.1 kHz?
Under the Nyquist sampling theorem, a 44.1 kHz sampling rate faithfully captures the entire $20 \text{ kHz}$ human hearing spectrum with a safety transition band.

#### 5. What frequency band does AM radio use in kHz?
AM radio broadcasting operates in the Medium Frequency (MF) band from **530 kHz to 1700 kHz** (with $10 \text{ kHz}$ channel spacing in North America).

#### 6. What is the difference between 44.1 kHz and 48 kHz audio?
- **44.1 kHz:** Standard sampling rate for CD audio and digital music streaming.
- **48 kHz:** Standard sampling rate for professional video, television broadcast, and film soundtrack production.

#### 7. What is ultrasonic frequency in kHz?
Acoustic sound frequencies above **20 kHz** are classified as ultrasonic (e.g., medical ultrasound imaging operates at $2,000 \text{ kHz} - 15,000 \text{ kHz}$ or $2-15 \text{ MHz}$).

#### 8. How do I convert kilohertz to hertz mentally?
Multiply by 1,000. For example, $5 \text{ kHz} = 5,000 \text{ Hz}$.
