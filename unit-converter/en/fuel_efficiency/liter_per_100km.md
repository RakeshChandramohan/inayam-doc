## Liters per 100 Kilometers (L/100 km) Fuel Efficiency Guide & Converter

### Definition
**Liters per 100 kilometers** (symbol: **L/100 km**) is a metric unit of automotive fuel consumption defined as **the volume of fuel in liters consumed to travel a distance of 100 kilometers**.

Unlike distance-per-volume metrics ($\text{km/L}$ or $\text{MPG}$), L/100 km measures **fuel volume per distance**. It is the official fuel economy standard used across Europe, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and China. Lower L/100 km numbers indicate superior fuel efficiency.

### How to Calculate L/100 km Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between $\text{L/100 km}$, $\text{km/L}$, and US $\text{MPG}$:

- **L/100 km to km/L:** $\text{km/L} = \frac{100}{\text{L/100 km}}$
- **L/100 km to US MPG:** $\text{US MPG} = \frac{235.214583}{\text{L/100 km}}$
- **L/100 km to UK Imperial MPG:** $\text{UK MPG} = \frac{282.480936}{\text{L/100 km}}$
- **km/L to L/100 km:** $\text{L/100 km} = \frac{100}{\text{km/L}}$

#### Calculation Example:
If a European sedan consumes **6.2 Liters per 100 km**, convert the fuel consumption rating to US MPG and km/L:

1. **US MPG Conversion:**  
   $$\text{US MPG} = \frac{235.2146}{6.2 \text{ L/100 km}} \approx 37.94 \text{ US MPG}$$

2. **km/L Conversion:**  
   $$\text{km/L} = \frac{100}{6.2 \text{ L/100 km}} \approx 16.13 \text{ km/L}$$

### Linear Volume-per-Distance Advantage
L/100 km is a linear unit that directly reflects fuel costs and carbon emissions:
- Reducing consumption from **10 L/100 km to 8 L/100 km** saves exactly **2 liters** of fuel every 100 km.
- In contrast, improving MPG from **10 to 12 MPG** saves far more fuel than improving from **30 to 32 MPG** (the "MPG illusion").

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How do I convert L/100 km to US MPG?
Divide **235.215** by the L/100 km value: $\text{US MPG} = \frac{235.215}{\text{L/100 km}}$. For example, $\frac{235.215}{5.0 \text{ L/100 km}} = 47.04 \text{ US MPG}$.

#### 2. How do I convert L/100 km to km/L?
Divide 100 by the L/100 km value: $\text{km/L} = \frac{100}{\text{L/100 km}}$. For example, $\frac{100}{5 \text{ L/100 km}} = 20 \text{ km/L}$.

#### 3. Is a lower or higher L/100 km number better for fuel efficiency?
A **lower** L/100 km number is better because it means the vehicle burns fewer liters of gas to travel 100 kilometers.

#### 4. What is a good fuel economy rating in L/100 km?
A rating below **6.0 L/100 km** ($\approx 39 \text{ US MPG}$) is considered good, while hybrid vehicles achieve **3.5 L/100 km to 4.5 L/100 km**.

#### 5. What is 30 US MPG in L/100 km?
30 US MPG equals approximately **7.84 L/100 km** ($\frac{235.215}{30}$).

#### 6. What is 50 US MPG in L/100 km?
50 US MPG equals approximately **4.70 L/100 km** ($\frac{235.215}{50}$).

#### 7. Why is L/100 km used in Europe instead of MPG?
L/100 km provides a direct linear measurement of fuel volume used and $\text{CO}_2$ emissions per distance, avoiding the "MPG illusion."

#### 8. How do I calculate total fuel used for a trip using L/100 km?
Multiply trip distance in kilometers by L/100 km and divide by 100: $\text{Fuel Used (L)} = \frac{\text{Distance (km)} \times \text{L/100 km}}{100}$.
