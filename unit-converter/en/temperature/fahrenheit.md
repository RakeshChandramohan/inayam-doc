## Fahrenheit (°F) Measurement Guide & Temperature Converter

### Definition
**Fahrenheit** (symbol: **°F**) is a temperature scale predominantly used in the United States, its territories, the Bahamas, Belize, and the Cayman Islands. 

Under standard atmospheric pressure, the Fahrenheit scale defines the freezing point of water at **$32^\circ\text{F}$** and the boiling point of water at **$212^\circ\text{F}$**, dividing the interval between freezing and boiling into exactly **180 equal degrees**.

### How to Calculate Fahrenheit Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between Fahrenheit, Celsius, and Kelvin:

- **Fahrenheit to Celsius:** $^\circ\text{C} = (^\circ\text{F} - 32) \times \frac{5}{9} = \frac{^\circ\text{F} - 32}{1.8}$
- **Fahrenheit to Kelvin:** $\text{K} = (^\circ\text{F} - 32) \times \frac{5}{9} + 273.15$
- **Celsius to Fahrenheit:** $^\circ\text{F} = (^\circ\text{C} \times 1.8) + 32$

#### Calculation Example:
If a summer weather forecast reports a outdoor temperature of **$86^\circ\text{F}$**, convert the temperature to Celsius:

$$^\circ\text{C} = \frac{86 - 32}{1.8} = \frac{54}{1.8} = 30^\circ\text{C}$$

### Historical Origin by Daniel Gabriel Fahrenheit
The scale was established in 1724 by German physicist **Daniel Gabriel Fahrenheit**. He calibrated $0^\circ\text{F}$ using an equal mixture of ice, water, and ammonium chloride brine, $32^\circ\text{F}$ as the ice-water freezing point, and initially $96^\circ\text{F}$ as human body temperature.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. What is the formula to convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?
Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature and divide by 1.8: $^\circ\text{C} = (^\circ\text{F} - 32) / 1.8$.

#### 2. What is the freezing point and boiling point of water in Fahrenheit?
Under standard sea-level pressure, water freezes at **$32^\circ\text{F}$** and boils at **$212^\circ\text{F}$**.

#### 3. How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius mentally?
Subtract 30 from the Fahrenheit temperature and divide by 2. For example, $80^\circ\text{F} - 30 = 50 / 2 \approx 25^\circ\text{C}$ (exact is $26.67^\circ\text{C}$).

#### 4. At what temperature do Fahrenheit and Celsius read the same value?
Fahrenheit and Celsius are identical at **$-40^\circ$** ($-40^\circ\text{F} = -40^\circ\text{C}$).

#### 5. What is normal body temperature in Fahrenheit?
Normal average human body temperature is approximately **$98.6^\circ\text{F}$** ($37^\circ\text{C}$).

#### 6. What is absolute zero in Fahrenheit?
Absolute zero is **$-459.67^\circ\text{F}$** ($0\text{ K}$ or $-273.15^\circ\text{C}$).

#### 7. Why does the US still use the Fahrenheit scale?
The US retained Fahrenheit due to historical infrastructure, weather broadcasting conventions, and fine-grained 1-degree resolution for human thermal comfort.

#### 8. How many degrees separate freezing and boiling water in Fahrenheit?
There are exactly **180 degrees** between water freezing ($32^\circ\text{F}$) and water boiling ($212^\circ\text{F}$).
