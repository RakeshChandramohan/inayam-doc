## Cubic Meter (m³) Measurement Guide & Volume Converter

### Definition
The **cubic meter** (symbol: **m³** or **cu m**) is the official SI derived unit of volume equal to the volume of a cube with sides measuring **1 meter** in length.

One cubic meter equals exactly **1,000 liters** ($1,000 \text{ L}$), **1,000,000 cubic centimeters** ($10^6 \text{ cm}^3$), or approximately **264.172 US liquid gallons**. Cubic meters are used globally to measure residential water utility consumption, concrete volume in construction engineering, cargo shipping container volume, gas utility flow rates, and reservoir water capacities.

### How to Calculate Cubic Meter Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between cubic meters and other volume units:

- **Cubic Meters to Liters:** $\text{Liters} = \text{m}^3 \times 1,000$
- **Cubic Meters to US Gallons:** $\text{US gal} = \text{m}^3 \times 264.17205$
- **Cubic Meters to Cubic Feet:** $\text{cu ft} = \text{m}^3 \times 35.314667$
- **Cubic Meters to Cubic Yards:** $\text{cu yd} = \text{m}^3 \times 1.3079506$

#### Calculation Example:
If a residential swimming pool holds **45 cubic meters** of water, convert the volume to liters and US gallons:

1. **Liters Conversion:**  
   $$45 \text{ m}^3 \times 1,000 \text{ L/m}^3 = 45,000 \text{ liters}$$

2. **US Gallons Conversion:**  
   $$45 \text{ m}^3 \times 264.172 \text{ gal/m}^3 \approx 11,887.74 \text{ US gallons}$$

### Water Density & Weight Benchmark: 1 m³ Water = 1 Metric Tonne
Under standard laboratory conditions ($4^\circ\text{C}$), **1 cubic meter of pure water** has a mass of exactly **1,000 kilograms** (**1 metric tonne** or 2,204.62 lbs).

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many liters are in 1 cubic meter (m³)?
There are exactly **1,000 liters** in 1 cubic meter.

#### 2. How many US gallons are in 1 cubic meter?
One cubic meter equals approximately **264.172 US liquid gallons**.

#### 3. How many cubic feet are in 1 cubic meter?
One cubic meter equals approximately **35.3147 cubic feet**.

#### 4. How many cubic yards are in 1 cubic meter?
One cubic meter equals approximately **1.30795 cubic yards**.

#### 5. What is the weight of 1 cubic meter of water in metric tonnes?
One cubic meter of pure water weighs exactly **1 metric tonne** ($1,000 \text{ kg}$ or $2,204.62 \text{ lbs}$).

#### 6. What is a 20-foot shipping container volume in cubic meters?
A standard 20-foot intermodal shipping container has an internal volume of approximately **33.2 cubic meters** (1,172 cu ft).

#### 7. How do I convert liters to cubic meters mentally?
Divide the liters by 1,000. For example, $5,000 \text{ liters} / 1,000 = 5 \text{ m}^3$.

#### 8. How many cubic centimeters (cm³) make 1 cubic meter?
There are exactly **1,000,000 cubic centimeters** ($100 \text{ cm} \times 100 \text{ cm} \times 100 \text{ cm}$) in 1 cubic meter.
