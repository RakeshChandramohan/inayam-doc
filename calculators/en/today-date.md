### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. What day of the week is today?
The tool automatically detects your device's date and displays the exact day of the week (e.g., Thursday).

#### 2. What week number of the year is today?
In ISO 8601 standards, week numbers range from Week 1 to Week 52 (or 53), starting on the first Monday of the year.

#### 3. How many days are left in the current calendar year?
In a standard 365-day year, remaining days equal $365 - \text{Day of Year}$. In a leap year, it equals $366 - \text{Day of Year}$.

#### 4. What is the difference between Gregorian and Julian calendars?
The modern Gregorian calendar introduced leap year rules (skipping century leap years not divisible by 400), correcting the 11-minute annual drift of the older Julian calendar.
