## Watt (W) Power Measurement Guide & Power Converter

### Definition
The **watt** (symbol: **W**) is the official SI unit of power, representing the rate of energy consumption, conversion, or generation over time. One watt is defined as **one joule of energy transferred per second** ($1 \text{ W} = 1 \text{ J/s} = 1 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2\cdot\text{s}^{-3}$).

In electrical systems, 1 watt is produced when a current of 1 ampere flows across an electric potential of 1 volt ($P = V \times I$).

### How to Calculate Watt Power Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between watts and other power metrics:

- **Watts to Kilowatts:** $\text{kW} = \text{Watts} / 1,000$
- **Watts to Horsepower (hp):** $\text{hp} = \text{Watts} / 745.69987$
- **Watts to BTU/hr:** $\text{BTU/h} = \text{Watts} \times 3.41214163$
- **Electric Power Formula:** $P (\text{Watts}) = V (\text{Volts}) \times I (\text{Amperes})$

#### Calculation Example:
If an electric room heater operates at **1,500 Watts** on a **120-volt** power circuit:

1. **Kilowatt Conversion:**  
   $$1,500 \text{ W} / 1,000 = 1.5 \text{ kW}$$

2. **Electrical Current Draw (Amperes):**  
   $$I = \frac{P}{V} = \frac{1,500 \text{ W}}{120 \text{ V}} = 12.5 \text{ Amperes}$$

### James Watt History & Industrial Revolution Origin
The unit was named by the British Association for the Advancement of Science in 1882 to honor Scottish inventor **James Watt**, whose revolutionary improvements to the steam engine powered the Industrial Revolution and established the baseline measurement of mechanical power.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many watts are in 1 kilowatt (kW)?
There are exactly 1,000 watts in 1 kilowatt.

#### 2. How many watts equal 1 horsepower (hp)?
One mechanical horsepower equals approximately **745.7 watts** ($0.7457 \text{ kW}$).

#### 3. What is the difference between a Watt and a Watt-Hour?
- **Watt ($\text{W}$):** Measures power output or rate of energy flow.
- **Watt-Hour ($\text{Wh}$):** Measures total cumulative energy consumed over time ($\text{Power} \times \text{Time}$).

#### 4. How do I calculate appliance electricity consumption in kWh?
Multiply wattage by daily operating hours and divide by 1,000: $\text{kWh} = \frac{\text{Watts} \times \text{Hours}}{1,000}$.

#### 5. How many BTU per hour are in 1 watt?
One watt equals approximately 3.41214 BTU/h (British Thermal Units per hour). Air conditioner cooling capacity rated at 12,000 BTU/h equals ~3,517 watts.

#### 6. What is the electrical power formula ($P = V \times I$)?
Electric power in watts equals voltage in volts multiplied by current in amperes ($P = V \cdot I$).

#### 7. How many watts make 1 Megawatt (MW)?
One megawatt equals exactly 1,000,000 watts ($1,000 \text{ kW}$).

#### 8. How do I convert watts to kilowatts mentally?
Divide the wattage by 1,000 (move the decimal point 3 places to the left). For example, $2,500 \text{ W} = 2.5 \text{ kW}$.
