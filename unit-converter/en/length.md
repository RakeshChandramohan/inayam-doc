### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. What is the SI base unit for length?
The International System of Units (SI) base unit for length is the **meter (m)**.

#### 2. How many feet are in 1 meter?
One meter equals approximately **3.28084 feet** ($\approx 3\text{ ft } 3.37\text{ in}$).

#### 3. How many kilometers are in 1 statute mile?
One statute mile equals exactly **1.609344 kilometers**.

#### 4. How many centimeters are in 1 inch?
One inch equals exactly **2.54 centimeters**.

#### 5. How many millimeters are in 1 inch?
One inch equals exactly **25.4 millimeters**.

#### 6. How many yards are in 1 mile?
There are exactly **1,760 yards** ($5,280 \text{ feet}$) in 1 mile.

#### 7. What is a Light-Year?
A light-year is the astronomical distance light travels in one Julian year ($\approx 9.46 \times 10^{12} \text{ km}$ or $5.88 \times 10^{12} \text{ miles}$).

#### 8. How do I convert kilometers to miles mentally?
Multiply kilometers by 0.62 (or divide by 1.6). For example, $100 \text{ km} \times 0.62 = 62 \text{ miles}$.
