### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. What is the SI base unit for speed?
The International System of Units (SI) derived unit for speed is the **meter per second (m/s)**.

#### 2. How do I convert m/s to km/h?
Multiply the speed in m/s by **3.6**: $\text{km/h} = \text{m/s} \times 3.6$.

#### 3. How many km/h are in 1 mph?
One mile per hour equals exactly **1.609344 km/h**.

#### 4. How many mph are in 100 km/h?
100 kilometers per hour equals approximately **62.14 mph**.

#### 5. What is the speed of sound (Mach 1) in mph and km/h?
The speed of sound in dry air at $20^\circ\text{C}$ is approximately **767 mph** ($1,235 \text{ km/h}$ or $343 \text{ m/s}$).

#### 6. What is the speed of light in vacuum?
The speed of light in vacuum ($c$) is defined as exactly **299,792,458 m/s** ($\approx 1.079 \text{ billion km/h}$ or $670.6 \text{ million mph}$).

#### 7. What is a Knot in speed measurement?
A knot is a nautical unit of speed equal to **1 nautical mile per hour** ($1.15078 \text{ mph}$ or $1.852 \text{ km/h}$).

#### 8. How do I convert mph to km/h mentally?
Multiply by 1.6. For example, $50 \text{ mph} \times 1.6 = 80 \text{ km/h}$.
