## Mile per Gallon (MPG) Fuel Efficiency Guide & Fuel Economy Converter

### Definition
**Miles per gallon** (symbol: **mpg** or **MPG**) is an imperial and US customary unit of automotive fuel efficiency defined as **the distance in statute miles a vehicle travels per single gallon of fuel consumed**.

Under US standards, **1 US MPG** uses a US liquid gallon ($3.785 \text{ L}$). Under UK standards, **1 Imperial MPG** uses a UK Imperial gallon ($4.546 \text{ L}$). MPG is the official fuel economy rating required on EPA window stickers across North America and VCA tags in the UK.

### How to Calculate MPG Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between US MPG, UK Imperial MPG, $\text{km/L}$, and $\text{L/100 km}$:

- **US MPG to Kilometers per Liter (km/L):** $\text{km/L} = \text{US MPG} \times 0.42514371$
- **US MPG to UK Imperial MPG:** $\text{UK MPG} = \text{US MPG} \times 1.20095042$
- **US MPG to Liters per 100 km (L/100 km):** $\text{L/100 km} = \frac{235.214583}{\text{US MPG}}$
- **km/L to US MPG:** $\text{US MPG} = \text{km/L} / 0.42514371$

#### Calculation Example:
If an automobile EPA rating is **38 US MPG**, convert the fuel economy rating to km/L and L/100 km:

1. **km/L Conversion:**  
   $$\text{km/L} = 38 \text{ US MPG} \times 0.425144 \approx 16.16 \text{ km/L}$$

2. **Liters per 100 km Conversion:**  
   $$\text{L/100 km} = \frac{235.2146}{38 \text{ US MPG}} \approx 6.19 \text{ L/100 km}$$

### US MPG vs. UK Imperial MPG Variance
- **US Gallon:** $3.78541 \text{ liters}$.
- **UK Imperial Gallon:** $4.54609 \text{ liters}$ ($\approx 20\%$ larger).  
*This means a UK Imperial MPG rating is approximately **20.1% higher** than the equivalent US MPG rating ($1 \text{ US MPG} = 1.201 \text{ UK MPG}$).*

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How do I convert US MPG to km/L?
Multiply US MPG by **0.425144**. For example, $30 \text{ US MPG} \times 0.425144 = 12.75 \text{ km/L}$.

#### 2. How do I convert US MPG to L/100 km?
Divide 235.215 by US MPG: $\text{L/100 km} = \frac{235.215}{\text{US MPG}}$. For example, $\frac{235.215}{35 \text{ MPG}} = 6.72 \text{ L/100 km}$.

#### 3. What is the difference between US MPG and UK Imperial MPG?
An **Imperial gallon** ($4.546 \text{ L}$) is 20% larger than a **US liquid gallon** ($3.785 \text{ L}$), so 1 US MPG equals 1.201 UK Imperial MPG.

#### 4. What is MPGe (Miles per Gallon Equivalent)?
**MPGe** measures Electric Vehicle (EV) energy efficiency, where $33.7 \text{ kWh}$ of electricity is defined by the US EPA as equal to the energy content of 1 gallon of gasoline.

#### 5. What is a good EPA MPG rating for a gas car?
A combined city/highway rating above **30 US MPG** is considered good for gas cars, while hybrid cars routinely achieve **45 US MPG to 55 US MPG**.

#### 6. What is 50 US MPG in L/100 km?
50 US MPG equals approximately **4.70 Liters per 100 km**.

#### 7. How do I calculate fuel efficiency in MPG manually?
Divide total miles driven by gallons consumed during refilling: $\text{MPG} = \frac{\text{Miles Driven}}{\text{Gallons Used}}$.

#### 8. How do I convert US MPG to km/L mentally?
Divide US MPG by 2.35. For example, $40 \text{ MPG} / 2.35 \approx 17 \text{ km/L}$.
