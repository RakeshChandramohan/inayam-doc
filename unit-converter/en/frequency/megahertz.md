## Megahertz (MHz) Frequency Guide & Frequency Converter

### Definition
The **megahertz** (symbol: **MHz**) is a metric unit of frequency equal to **1,000,000 hertz** ($10^6 \text{ Hz}$) or **1,000 kilohertz** ($1,000 \text{ kHz}$).

Megahertz is used universally across FM radio broadcasting ($88\text{MHz} - 108\text{MHz}$), legacy microprocessor CPU clock speeds, RAM memory module speeds (DDR4/DDR5 $\text{MT/s}$), cellular network bands (3G/4G $\text{700MHz} - 2600\text{MHz}$), and MRI medical diagnostic imaging scanners.

### How to Calculate Megahertz Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between megahertz and hertz or gigahertz:

- **Megahertz to Hertz:** $\text{Hz} = \text{MHz} \times 1,000,000$
- **Megahertz to Kilohertz:** $\text{kHz} = \text{MHz} \times 1,000$
- **Megahertz to Gigahertz:** $\text{GHz} = \text{MHz} / 1,000$
- **Period Time ($T$):** $T (\text{μs}) = \frac{1}{f (\text{MHz})}$

#### Calculation Example:
If an FM radio station broadcasts on a frequency of **101.5 MHz**, convert the carrier frequency to Hertz and calculate its radio wavelength ($\lambda$) in meters ($c \approx 3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$):

1. **Hertz Conversion:**  
   $$101.5 \text{ MHz} \times 1,000,000 = 101,500,000 \text{ Hz}$$

2. **Wavelength Calculation:**  
   $$\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = \frac{299,792,458 \text{ m/s}}{101,500,000 \text{ Hz}} \approx 2.95 \text{ meters}$$

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many hertz are in 1 megahertz (MHz)?
There are exactly **1,000,000 hertz** ($10^6 \text{ Hz}$) in 1 megahertz.

#### 2. How many kilohertz (kHz) are in 1 megahertz?
There are exactly **1,000 kilohertz** in 1 megahertz.

#### 3. How many megahertz equal 1 gigahertz (GHz)?
There are exactly **1,000 megahertz** in 1 gigahertz.

#### 4. What frequency range does FM radio use in MHz?
Commercial FM radio broadcasting operates from **88 MHz to 108 MHz** worldwide.

#### 5. What is the wavelength of a 100 MHz radio wave?
A 100 MHz radio wave has a wavelength of approximately **3 meters** ($c / f = 3 \times 10^8 / 10^8$).

#### 6. What is DDR RAM MT/s vs MHz?
RAM speeds listed as "DDR4-3200 MHz" actually run at a clock speed of **1600 MHz** because Double Data Rate (DDR) transfers data twice per clock cycle ($3,000 \text{ MT/s}$).

#### 7. What is the frequency of medical MRI scanners in MHz?
1.5-Tesla MRI machines resonate hydrogen protons at approximately **63.8 MHz** (Larmor frequency). 3-Tesla MRI machines resonate at **127.7 MHz**.

#### 8. How do I convert megahertz to gigahertz mentally?
Divide by 1,000 (move decimal point 3 places left). For example, $2,400 \text{ MHz} = 2.4 \text{ GHz}$.
