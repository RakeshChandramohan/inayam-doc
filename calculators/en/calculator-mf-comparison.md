## Mutual Fund (MF) Comparison Calculator – Direct vs. Regular Plans

### What is a Mutual Fund Comparison Calculator?
A **Mutual Fund (MF) Comparison Calculator** evaluates the long-term wealth impact of expense ratio differences between **Direct Plans** and **Regular Plans** in mutual funds.

Because **Direct Plans** are bought directly from mutual fund asset management companies (AMCs) without broker commissions, their annual expense ratios are typically **0.5% to 1.5% lower** than **Regular Plans**. Over 10 to 20-year horizons, this lower expense ratio translates into significantly higher compound returns for investors.

---

### Direct vs. Regular Mutual Fund Plans Comparison

| Feature | Direct Mutual Fund Plan | Regular Mutual Fund Plan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intermediary Commission** | Zero distributor commission | Annual trailing commissions paid to brokers |
| **Expense Ratio** | Lower (typically 0.1% – 0.8%) | Higher (typically 1.0% – 2.2%) |
| **Net Asset Value (NAV)** | Higher NAV over time | Lower NAV due to higher fee deductions |
| **Long-Term Returns** | Substantially higher (1.0%–1.5% extra per year) | Lower cumulative compounding wealth |

### Formula to Calculate Net Mutual Fund Returns

The compounded future value adjusted for expense ratio ($TER$) is:

$$
FV_{\text{Net}} = P \times \left(1 + r_{\text{gross}} - TER\right)^n
$$

Where:
- **P** = Initial principal investment amount
- **$r_{\text{gross}}$** = Gross annual portfolio market return rate
- **TER** = Total Expense Ratio of the mutual fund scheme (in decimal form)
- **n** = Investment horizon in years

#### Step-by-Step Numerical Comparison Example:
Suppose you invest **₹100,000** for **20 years** with an expected gross market return of **12.0%**:
- **Direct Plan Expense Ratio ($TER_{\text{Direct}}$):** 0.50% ($\text{Net Return} = 12.0\% - 0.50\% = 11.50\%$)
- **Regular Plan Expense Ratio ($TER_{\text{Regular}}$):** 1.75% ($\text{Net Return} = 12.0\% - 1.75\% = 10.25\%$)

1. **Direct Plan Future Value:**  
   $$FV_{\text{Direct}} = 100,000 \times (1 + 0.115)^{20} \approx ₹8,75,373$$

2. **Regular Plan Future Value:**  
   $$FV_{\text{Regular}} = 100,000 \times (1 + 0.1025)^{20} \approx ₹7,04,001$$

- **Net Wealth Difference:** Choosing the Direct Plan yields **₹1,71,372 extra profit** (+24.3% higher wealth) on a single ₹100,000 deposit!

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. What is an Expense Ratio in mutual funds?
An Expense Ratio represents the annual fee charged by a mutual fund company to manage your money, covering administrative, management, and marketing expenses.

#### 2. Can I switch my existing Regular Mutual Fund plans to Direct plans?
Yes. You can switch from Regular to Direct plans within the same fund house. Note that switching is treated as a redemption, which may attract exit loads or capital gains taxes if done within 1 year.

#### 3. Why are Direct plans better for long-term investors?
Because zero commission is paid to third parties, every rupee saved on expense ratios compounds directly in your mutual fund account over time.
