## Week (wk) Time Measurement Guide & Time Converter

### Definition
A **week** (symbol: **wk**) is a standard unit of time equal to **7 days**, **168 hours**, **10,080 minutes**, or exactly **604,800 seconds**.

The 7-day weekly calendar cycle is used globally for work schedule planning, corporate payroll reporting, school academic terms, sports league match fixtures, and pregnancy gestation tracking ($40 \text{ weeks}$).

### How to Calculate Week Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between weeks and days, hours, or minutes:

- **Weeks to Days:** $\text{Days} = \text{Weeks} \times 7$
- **Weeks to Hours:** $\text{Hours} = \text{Weeks} \times 168$
- **Weeks to Minutes:** $\text{Minutes} = \text{Weeks} \times 10,080$
- **Days to Weeks:** $\text{Weeks} = \text{Days} / 7$

#### Calculation Example:
If a software development sprint is scheduled for **3 weeks**, convert the sprint duration to days and working hours (assuming $40 \text{ hours}$ per work week):

1. **Days Conversion:**  
   $$3 \text{ weeks} \times 7 = 21 \text{ calendar days}$$

2. **Total Work Hours:**  
   $$3 \text{ weeks} \times 40 \text{ hours/week} = 120 \text{ working hours}$$

### ISO 8601 Week Date System Standard
The International Organization for Standardization (ISO 8601) defines Monday as the first day of the week, with **Week 1** defined as the week containing the first Thursday of the calendar year. A standard Gregorian calendar year has **52 weeks plus 1 day** (or 52 weeks plus 2 days in a leap year).

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many days are in 1 week?
There are exactly **7 days** in 1 week.

#### 2. How many hours are in 1 week?
There are exactly **168 hours** ($7 \times 24$) in 1 week.

#### 3. How many minutes are in 1 week?
There are exactly **10,080 minutes** ($168 \times 60$) in 1 week.

#### 4. How many seconds are in 1 week?
There are exactly **604,800 seconds** ($10,080 \times 60$) in 1 week.

#### 5. How many weeks are in 1 year?
A standard 365-day year contains **52 weeks plus 1 day** (52.1428 weeks).

#### 6. What is a "Fortnight"?
A fortnight is a traditional unit of time equal to **2 weeks** or **14 days** ($336 \text{ hours}$).

#### 7. How many weeks long is full-term human pregnancy pregnancy gestation?
A full-term human pregnancy is calculated as **40 weeks** (280 days), measured from the first day of the last menstrual period (LMP).

#### 8. What is ISO 8601 week numbering?
ISO 8601 defines Monday as day 1 of the week, with Week 01 defined as the week containing the first Thursday of January.
