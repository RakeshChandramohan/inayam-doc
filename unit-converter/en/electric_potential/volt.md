## Volt (V) Electric Potential Guide & Voltage Converter

### Definition
The **volt** (symbol: **V**) is the SI derived unit of electric potential, electric potential difference (voltage), and electromotive force. One volt is defined as **the electric potential difference between two points of a conducting wire when an electric current of one ampere dissipates one watt of power** ($1 \text{ V} = 1 \text{ W/A} = 1 \text{ J/C}$).

Volts are used universally across electrical engineering, battery cell specifications, power grid distribution, solar photovoltaic systems, and microelectronic circuit logic level design.

### How to Calculate Volt Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between volts and other electrical metrics:

- **Volts to Millivolts:** $\text{mV} = \text{Volts} \times 1,000$
- **Volts to Kilovolts:** $\text{kV} = \text{Volts} / 1,000$
- **Ohm's Law Voltage Formula:** $V (\text{Volts}) = I (\text{Amps}) \times R (\text{Ohms})$
- **Power Voltage Formula:** $V (\text{Volts}) = \frac{P (\text{Watts})}{I (\text{Amps})}$

#### Calculation Example:
If an industrial electric motor draws **20 Amperes** of current across a resistance of **11.5 Ohms**, calculate the voltage supply:

$$\text{Voltage (V)} = I \times R = 20 \text{ A} \times 11.5 \text{ }\Omega = 230 \text{ Volts}$$

### Alessandro Volta History
The unit was named by the International Electrical Congress in 1881 to honor Italian physicist **Alessandro Volta**, who invented the voltaic pile in 1799—the world's first chemical battery capable of producing a continuous electric current.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. What is the definition of 1 Volt?
One volt is defined as one joule of energy per coulomb of charge ($1 \text{ V} = 1 \text{ J/C} = 1 \text{ W/A}$).

#### 2. How many millivolts (mV) are in 1 volt (V)?
There are exactly 1,000 millivolts in 1 volt.

#### 3. What is household wall outlet voltage in North America vs Europe?
- **North America (US/Canada):** $120 \text{ V}$ ($60 \text{ Hz}$).
- **Europe / UK / Asia / Australia:** $230 \text{ V}$ ($50 \text{ Hz}$).

#### 4. How do I calculate voltage using Ohm's Law?
Multiply current in amperes by resistance in ohms: $V = I \cdot R$.

#### 5. What is the voltage of a standard AA battery?
A standard disposable AA or AAA alkaline battery provides **1.5 Volts** when fresh. Rechargeable NiMH batteries provide **1.2 Volts**.

#### 6. What is the voltage of a standard car battery?
A standard lead-acid automotive battery supplies **12.6 Volts** DC at rest ($\approx 13.8 - 14.4 \text{ V}$ when running on the alternator).

#### 7. How many volts make 1 Kilovolt (kV)?
There are exactly 1,000 volts in 1 kilovolt ($1,000 \text{ V}$). High-voltage electrical power transmission lines operate at $110 \text{ kV}$ to $765 \text{ kV}$.

#### 8. How do I convert millivolts to volts mentally?
Divide millivolts by 1,000. For example, $3,300 \text{ mV} = 3.3 \text{ V}$.
