## Kilometer per Liter (km/L) Fuel Efficiency Guide & Fuel Economy Converter

### Definition
**Kilometers per liter** (symbol: **km/L** or **km/l**) is a metric unit of automotive fuel efficiency defined as **the distance in kilometers a vehicle travels per single liter of fuel consumed**.

Kilometers per liter is the standard vehicle fuel economy metric used across Asia (India, Japan), South America, and Scandinavia. Higher km/L values indicate superior vehicle fuel economy.

### How to Calculate km/L Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between $\text{km/L}$, US $\text{mpg}$, UK $\text{mpg}$, and $\text{L/100 km}$:

- **km/L to US MPG:** $\text{US MPG} = \text{km/L} \times 2.35214583$
- **km/L to UK Imperial MPG:** $\text{UK MPG} = \text{km/L} \times 2.82480936$
- **km/L to Liters per 100 km (L/100 km):** $\text{L/100 km} = \frac{100}{\text{km/L}}$
- **US MPG to km/L:** $\text{km/L} = \text{US MPG} / 2.35214583$

#### Calculation Example:
If a hybrid automobile achieves a mileage rating of **22 km/L**, convert the fuel efficiency to US MPG and L/100 km:

1. **US MPG Conversion:**  
   $$\text{US MPG} = 22 \text{ km/L} \times 2.352146 \approx 51.75 \text{ US MPG}$$

2. **Liters per 100 km Conversion:**  
   $$\text{L/100 km} = \frac{100}{22 \text{ km/L}} \approx 4.55 \text{ L/100 km}$$

### Distance-Per-Volume vs. Volume-Per-Distance Variance
- **km/L & MPG (Distance per Volume):** Higher numbers represent better fuel efficiency.
- **L/100 km (Volume per Distance):** Lower numbers represent better fuel efficiency (inverse relationship).

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How do I convert km/L to US MPG?
Multiply the km/L value by **2.35215**. For example, $15 \text{ km/L} \times 2.35215 = 35.28 \text{ US MPG}$.

#### 2. How do I convert km/L to L/100 km?
Divide 100 by the km/L value: $\text{L/100 km} = \frac{100}{\text{km/L}}$. For example, $\frac{100}{20 \text{ km/L}} = 5.0 \text{ L/100 km}$.

#### 3. What is 20 km/L in US MPG?
20 km/L equals approximately **47.04 US MPG**.

#### 4. What is 15 km/L in UK Imperial MPG?
15 km/L equals approximately **42.37 UK Imperial MPG**.

#### 5. Is a higher km/L rating better for fuel economy?
Yes. Kilometers per liter measures distance traveled per unit of fuel; higher values mean the vehicle travels further per liter of gas.

#### 6. What is a good km/L rating for a passenger car?
A rating above **15 km/L** ($\approx 35 \text{ US MPG}$) is considered good, while hybrid vehicles routinely achieve **20 km/L to 25 km/L** ($\approx 47-59 \text{ US MPG}$).

#### 7. How do I calculate fuel efficiency in km/L manually?
Divide total kilometers driven by total liters consumed: $\text{km/L} = \frac{\text{Distance (km)}}{\text{Fuel Used (L)}}$.

#### 8. How do I convert km/L to US MPG mentally?
Multiply by 2.35 (or double the km/L value and add 35%). For example, $10 \text{ km/L} \times 2.35 = 23.5 \text{ MPG}$.
