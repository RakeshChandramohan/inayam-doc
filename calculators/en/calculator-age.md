## Age Calculator – Calculate Exact Age in Years, Months, Days, and Hours

### What is an Age Calculator?
An **Age Calculator** is an online utility that computes an individual's exact chronological age by calculating the precise difference between their date of birth (DOB) and a target reference date (such as today's date).

Using an **Online Age Calculator** eliminates manual leap-year math errors, providing exact breakdowns in total years, months, weeks, days, hours, and minutes for legal documentation, passport applications, milestone birthdays, and retirement planning.

---

### How Exact Chronological Age is Calculated

Calculating exact age requires adjusting for leap years (366 days), varying month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days), and time zone differences:

1. **Year Difference:** $\text{Target Year} - \text{Birth Year}$
2. **Month Adjustment:** If the target month/day precedes the birth month/day in the current calendar year, subtract 1 from the total years.
3. **Day Breakdown:** Calculate remaining days based on the exact calendar days of the preceding month.

#### Step-by-Step Numerical Example:
Suppose a person's Birth Date is **March 15, 1995**, and the target calculation date is **August 1, 2026**:
- **Total Years:** $2026 - 1995 = 31\text{ Years}$ (since Aug 1 is after Mar 15).
- **Total Months:** From March 15 to July 15 = **4 Months**.
- **Remaining Days:** From July 15 to August 1 = **17 Days** (July has 31 days).
- **Exact Age Output:** **31 Years, 4 Months, 17 Days**.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. How does the Age Calculator handle leap years?
The Age Calculator automatically detects leap years (years divisible by 4, except century years not divisible by 400) and adds an extra day (February 29) to ensure 100% mathematical accuracy.

#### 2. What is the age of a person born on February 29 (Leap Day)?
For non-leap years, official legal systems recognize February 29 birthdays on either **February 28** or **March 1**, depending on jurisdiction.

#### 3. Can I calculate my age on a future or past date?
Yes. By specifying a custom target date, you can calculate how old you will be on a future retirement date, wedding anniversary, or official exam cutoff date.

#### 4. Why is chronological age calculation important for official documents?
Government applications (passports, driver's licenses, voting registration, pension forms) require exact age verification on specific cutoff dates.
