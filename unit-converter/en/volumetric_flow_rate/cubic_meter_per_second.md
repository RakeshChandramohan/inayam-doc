## Cubic Meter per Second (m³/s) Volumetric Flow Rate Guide & Converter

### Definition
**Cubic meters per second** (symbol: **m³/s** or **$\text{m}^3\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$**) is the official SI derived unit of volumetric flow rate, defined as **the volume of one cubic meter of fluid passing through a given surface per second**.

One $\text{m}^3/\text{s}$ equals exactly **1,000 liters per second** ($1,000 \text{ L/s}$), **60,000 liters per minute** ($60,000 \text{ L/min}$), or approximately **15,850.32 US gallons per minute (GPM)**. Cubic meters per second is used universally across river discharge hydrography, dam spillway flood control, HVAC duct ventilation capacity, municipal water main supply engineering, and industrial pipeline hydraulics.

### How to Calculate m³/s Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between $\text{m}^3/\text{s}$ and $\text{L/s}$, $\text{GPM}$, and $\text{CFS}$:

- **m³/s to Liters per Second (L/s):** $\text{L/s} = \text{m}^3/\text{s} \times 1,000$
- **m³/s to US Gallons per Minute (GPM):** $\text{GPM} = \text{m}^3/\text{s} \times 15,850.3231$
- **m³/s to Cubic Feet per Second (CFS):** $\text{CFS} = \text{m}^3/\text{s} \times 35.314667$
- **Flow Continuity Equation:** $Q (\text{m}^3/\text{s}) = A (\text{m}^2) \times v (\text{m/s})$

#### Calculation Example:
If a hydro-electric power plant penstock pipe has a cross-sectional area ($A$) of **4 square meters** and water flows at a velocity ($v$) of **5 meters per second**, calculate the volumetric flow rate ($Q$) in $\text{m}^3/\text{s}$ and Liters per second:

1. **Flow Rate Calculation ($\text{m}^3/\text{s}$):**  
   $$Q = A \times v = 4 \text{ m}^2 \times 5 \text{ m/s} = 20 \text{ m}^3/\text{s}$$

2. **Liters per Second Conversion:**  
   $$\text{Flow Rate (L/s)} = 20 \text{ m}^3/\text{s} \times 1,000 = 20,000 \text{ L/s}$$

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. What is the definition of 1 m³/s?
One cubic meter per second represents a fluid flow rate of 1,000 liters passing a point every second.

#### 2. How many liters per second (L/s) are in 1 m³/s?
There are exactly **1,000 liters per second** in 1 m³/s.

#### 3. How many US gallons per minute (GPM) equal 1 m³/s?
One m³/s equals approximately **15,850.32 US gallons per minute**.

#### 4. How many cubic feet per second (CFS) equal 1 m³/s?
One m³/s equals approximately **35.3147 cubic feet per second**.

#### 5. How do I calculate volumetric flow rate using pipe diameter and velocity ($Q = A \cdot v$)?
Multiply the pipe cross-sectional area in $\text{m}^2$ ($\pi r^2$) by the fluid velocity in $\text{m/s}$.

#### 6. What is the flow rate of Niagara Falls in m³/s?
Niagara Falls flows at an average peak rate of approximately **2,400 m³/s** (over 700,000 US gallons per second).

#### 7. What is the flow rate of the Amazon River in m³/s?
The Amazon River discharges an average of **209,000 m³/s** into the Atlantic Ocean.

#### 8. How do I convert m³/s to liters per minute (L/min) mentally?
Multiply by 60,000. For example, $0.1 \text{ m}^3/\text{s} \times 60,000 = 6,000 \text{ L/min}$.
