## Fluid Ounce (fl oz) Measurement Guide & Volume Converter

### Definition
The **fluid ounce** (symbol: **fl oz**) is an imperial and US customary unit of liquid volume. 

Under US liquid standards, **1 US fluid ounce** equals $\frac{1}{128}$ of a US gallon, $\frac{1}{16}$ of a US pint, or exactly **29.5735295625 milliliters**. Under UK Imperial standards, **1 Imperial fluid ounce** equals $\frac{1}{160}$ of an Imperial gallon or exactly **28.4130625 milliliters**.

### How to Calculate Fluid Ounce Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between US fluid ounces and metric or imperial volume units:

- **US Fluid Ounces to Milliliters:** $\text{mL} = \text{fl oz} \times 29.5735296$
- **US Fluid Ounces to US Cups:** $\text{Cups} = \text{fl oz} / 8$
- **US Fluid Ounces to US Tablespoons:** $\text{tbsp} = \text{fl oz} \times 2$
- **Milliliters to US Fluid Ounces:** $\text{fl oz} = \text{mL} / 29.5735296$

#### Calculation Example:
If a beverage can contains **12 US fluid ounces**, convert the volume to milliliters:

$$\text{Volume (mL)} = 12 \text{ fl oz} \times 29.5735 \text{ mL/fl oz} \approx 354.88 \text{ milliliters (355 mL)}$$

### US Fluid Ounce vs. Imperial Fluid Ounce Variance
- **1 US Fluid Ounce:** Equals **29.5735 mL** ($\frac{1}{128} \text{ US gal}$).
- **1 Imperial Fluid Ounce:** Equals **28.4131 mL** ($\frac{1}{160} \text{ Imperial gal}$).  
*While 1 US fluid ounce is slightly larger (~4%) than 1 Imperial fluid ounce, a US pint ($16 \text{ fl oz} = 473 \text{ mL}$) is significantly smaller than a UK Imperial pint ($20 \text{ imp fl oz} = 568 \text{ mL}$).*

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many milliliters are in 1 US fluid ounce?
One US fluid ounce equals approximately **29.5735 milliliters** ($\approx 30 \text{ mL}$).

#### 2. How many milliliters are in 1 UK Imperial fluid ounce?
One UK Imperial fluid ounce equals approximately **28.4131 milliliters**.

#### 3. How many fluid ounces are in 1 US cup?
There are exactly **8 US fluid ounces** in 1 US customary cup.

#### 4. How many tablespoons are in 1 US fluid ounce?
There are exactly **2 US tablespoons** (or 6 US teaspoons) in 1 US fluid ounce.

#### 5. How many fluid ounces are in 1 US pint?
There are exactly **16 US fluid ounces** in 1 US liquid pint.

#### 6. How many fluid ounces are in 1 liter?
One liter equals approximately **33.814 US fluid ounces**.

#### 7. What is the difference between a Fluid Ounce and a Dry Weight Ounce?
- **Fluid Ounce ($\text{fl oz}$):** Measures liquid volume.
- **Avoirdupois Weight Ounce ($\text{oz}$):** Measures solid mass ($1 \text{ oz} = 28.3495 \text{ grams}$).

#### 8. How do I convert fluid ounces to milliliters mentally?
Multiply by 30. For example, $8 \text{ fl oz} \times 30 = 240 \text{ mL}$ (exact is $236.59 \text{ mL}$).
