## Kilocalorie (kcal / Food Calorie) Guide & Energy Converter

### Definition
The **kilocalorie** (symbol: **kcal**), commonly capitalized as a **Calorie** (with a capital "C") on nutritional labels, is a unit of energy equal to **1,000 small calories** ($1,000 \text{ cal}$) or exactly **4,184 joules** ($4.184 \text{ kilojoules}$).

One kilocalorie is defined as the amount of heat energy required to raise the temperature of **1 kilogram of pure water by 1 degree Celsius** (from $14.5^\circ\text{C}$ to $15.5^\circ\text{C}$) at standard atmospheric pressure.

### How to Calculate Kilocalorie Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between kilocalories and kilojoules or joules:

- **Kilocalories to Kilojoules:** $\text{kJ} = \text{kcal} \times 4.184$
- **Kilocalories to Joules:** $\text{Joules} = \text{kcal} \times 4,184$
- **Kilocalories to Watt-Hours:** $\text{Wh} = \text{kcal} \times 1.162222$
- **Kilojoules to Kilocalories:** $\text{kcal} = \text{kJ} / 4.184$

#### Calculation Example:
If a food product nutritional facts panel lists **650 kcal**, convert the dietary energy value to kilojoules:

$$\text{Energy (kJ)} = 650 \text{ kcal} \times 4.184 \text{ kJ/kcal} = 2,719.6 \text{ kilojoules}$$

### Macro Atwater Calorie Values
In human nutrition, macronutrient calorie density is defined using Atwater factors:
- **Proteins:** 4 kcal per gram ($16.7 \text{ kJ/g}$)
- **Carbohydrates:** 4 kcal per gram ($16.7 \text{ kJ/g}$)
- **Fats:** 9 kcal per gram ($37.7 \text{ kJ/g}$)
- **Alcohol:** 7 kcal per gram ($29.3 \text{ kJ/g}$)

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. What is the difference between a small calorie (cal) and a large Calorie (kcal)?
- **Small calorie ($\text{cal}$):** Energy required to heat 1 gram of water by $1^\circ\text{C}$ ($4.184 \text{ J}$).
- **Large Calorie ($\text{kcal}$ / Calorie with capital C):** Energy required to heat 1 kilogram of water by $1^\circ\text{C}$ ($1 \text{ kcal} = 1,000 \text{ cal} = 4.184 \text{ kJ}$).

#### 2. How many kilojoules (kJ) are in 1 food Calorie (kcal)?
One food Calorie (kilocalorie) equals exactly **4.184 kilojoules**.

#### 3. How many joules are in 1 kilocalorie (kcal)?
There are exactly **4,184 joules** in 1 kilocalorie.

#### 4. How many kilocalories (kcal) equal 2,000 kilojoules (kJ)?
Divide 2,000 by 4.184: $2,000 \text{ kJ} / 4.184 \approx 478 \text{ kcal}$.

#### 5. What is the recommended daily calorie intake for adults?
An average adult daily energy requirement is approximately **2,000 kcal** for women and **2,500 kcal** for men ($8,368 \text{ kJ} - 10,460 \text{ kJ}$).

#### 6. How many calories are in 1 pound of body fat?
One pound ($0.4536 \text{ kg}$) of stored human body fat contains approximately **3,500 kcal** ($14,644 \text{ kJ}$).

#### 7. How many calories do proteins, carbs, and fats contain per gram?
Carbohydrates and proteins contain **4 kcal/g**, fats contain **9 kcal/g**, and alcohol contains **7 kcal/g**.

#### 8. How do I convert food calories (kcal) to kilojoules (kJ) mentally?
Multiply by 4.2. For example, $500 \text{ kcal} \times 4.2 = 2,100 \text{ kJ}$ (exact is $2,092 \text{ kJ}$).
