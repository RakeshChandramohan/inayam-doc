### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. What is the SI derived unit for volume?
The SI derived unit for volume is the **cubic meter (m³)**.

#### 2. How many liters are in 1 US gallon?
One US liquid gallon equals exactly **3.785411784 liters** ($\approx 3.785 \text{ L}$).

#### 3. How many liters are in 1 UK Imperial gallon?
One UK Imperial gallon equals exactly **4.54609 liters**.

#### 4. How many milliliters are in 1 US fluid ounce?
One US fluid ounce equals approximately **29.5735 milliliters** ($\approx 30 \text{ mL}$).

#### 5. How many cups are in 1 US gallon?
There are exactly **16 US cups** ($4 \text{ quarts} \times 4 \text{ cups}$) in 1 US gallon.

#### 6. What is the difference between a US Cup and a Metric Cup?
A US customary cup equals **236.588 mL**, whereas an international metric cup equals exactly **250 mL**.

#### 7. What is the weight of 1 liter of pure water?
At standard temperature ($4^\circ\text{C}$), 1 liter of pure water has a mass of exactly **1 kilogram** ($2.2046 \text{ lbs}$).

#### 8. How do I convert liters to US gallons mentally?
Multiply liters by 0.264 (or divide by 3.8). For example, $10 \text{ L} / 3.8 \approx 2.63 \text{ gallons}$.
