## Terabyte (TB) Measurement Guide & Data Storage Converter

### Definition
A **terabyte** (symbol: **TB**) is a metric unit of digital information storage equal to **$1,000,000,000,000$ bytes** ($10^{12}$ bytes or **1,000 gigabytes**) under SI decimal standards, or **$1,099,511,627,776$ bytes** ($2^{40}$ bytes or **1,024 gigabytes**) in binary computing conventions.

Terabytes are used to measure external hard drive capacities, server backup arrays, cloud storage plans (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive), 4K/8K video production archives, and enterprise data center volumes.

### How to Calculate Terabyte Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between terabytes and other digital storage units:

- **Terabytes to Gigabytes:** $\text{GB} = \text{TB} \times 1,000$
- **Terabytes to Megabytes:** $\text{MB} = \text{TB} \times 1,000,000$
- **Terabytes to Petabytes:** $\text{PB} = \text{TB} / 1,000$
- **Terabytes to Bytes:** $\text{Bytes} = \text{TB} \times 10^{12}$

#### Calculation Example:
If a backup server drive has a capacity of **4 TB**, convert the storage capacity to Gigabytes and Megabytes:

1. **Gigabytes Conversion:**  
   $$4 \text{ TB} \times 1,000 = 4,000 \text{ GB}$$

2. **Megabytes Conversion:**  
   $$4 \text{ TB} \times 1,000,000 = 4,000,000 \text{ MB}$$

### Decimal (SI) vs. Binary (IEC) Storage Variance
Drive manufacturers label hard drives using decimal SI units ($1 \text{ TB} = 1,000,000,000,000 \text{ bytes}$). Operating systems like Windows calculate in binary tebibytes ($2^{40} = 1,099,511,627,776 \text{ bytes}$).  
*This is why a labeled 1 TB hard drive displays as approximately **~931 GB / GiB** in Windows Disk Management.*

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many gigabytes (GB) are in 1 terabyte (TB)?
In standard SI decimal units, 1 TB equals **1,000 GB**. In binary operating system conventions, 1 TB equals **1,024 GB (GiB)**.

#### 2. How many megabytes (MB) are in a terabyte (TB)?
There are **1,000,000 megabytes** in 1 decimal terabyte ($1,024 \times 1,024 = 1,048,576 \text{ MiB}$).

#### 3. How many photos can a 1 TB hard drive hold?
A 1 TB hard drive can hold approximately **250,000 high-resolution smartphone photos** (at ~4 MB per photo).

#### 4. How many hours of 4K video can fit in 1 TB?
1 TB holds approximately **50 hours** of compressed 4K UHD video (at ~20 GB per hour).

#### 5. Why does my 1 TB hard drive show as 931 GB in Windows?
Windows displays storage in binary Tebibytes ($\text{GiB}$). Divide $1,000,000,000,000 \text{ bytes} / 1,073,741,824 = 931.32 \text{ GiB}$.

#### 6. What is the difference between a Terabyte (TB) and a Tebibyte (TiB)?
- **Terabyte (TB):** Based on powers of 10 ($10^{12} = 1,000,000,000,000 \text{ bytes}$).
- **Tebibyte (TiB):** Based on powers of 2 ($2^{40} = 1,099,511,627,776 \text{ bytes}$).

#### 7. How many terabytes are in a petabyte (PB)?
There are **1,000 terabytes** in 1 decimal petabyte.

#### 8. How do I convert terabytes to gigabytes mentally?
Multiply the number of terabytes by 1,000. For example, $8 \text{ TB} = 8,000 \text{ GB}$.
