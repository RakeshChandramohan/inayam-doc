## Hour (h) Time Measurement Guide & Time Converter

### Definition
An **hour** (symbol: **h** or **hr**) is a unit of time equal to **60 minutes**, **3,600 seconds**, or $\frac{1}{24}$ of a standard solar day.

Hours are universally used for daily scheduling, work shift tracking, flight durations, project deadline management, astronomical observations, and velocity calculations ($\text{km/h}$, $\text{mph}$).

### How to Calculate Hour Conversions (Formulas & Examples)

To convert between hours and other time metrics:

- **Hours to Minutes:** $\text{Minutes} = \text{Hours} \times 60$
- **Hours to Seconds:** $\text{Seconds} = \text{Hours} \times 3,600$
- **Hours to Days:** $\text{Days} = \text{Hours} / 24$
- **Hours to Weeks:** $\text{Weeks} = \text{Hours} / 168$

#### Calculation Example:
If a flight duration is **7.5 hours**, convert the travel time to minutes and seconds:

1. **Minutes Conversion:**  
   $$7.5 \text{ h} \times 60 \text{ min/h} = 450 \text{ minutes}$$

2. **Seconds Conversion:**  
   $$7.5 \text{ h} \times 3,600 \text{ s/h} = 27,000 \text{ seconds}$$

### Etymology and Historical Origin
The word **hour** stems from the Anglo-Norman *houre* and Latin *hora*, derived from the Greek *hōra* (meaning "season" or "time of day"). Ancient Egyptians were the first to divide daytime and nighttime into 12 equal seasonal segments, establishing the modern 24-hour day cycle.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. How many minutes are in 1 hour?
There are exactly 60 minutes in 1 hour.

#### 2. How many seconds are in 1 hour?
There are exactly 3,600 seconds ($60 \times 60$) in 1 hour.

#### 3. How many hours are in a full day?
There are exactly 24 hours in a standard solar day.

#### 4. How many hours are in a 7-day week?
There are exactly **168 hours** ($24 \times 7$) in one week.

#### 5. How many work hours are in a standard full-time year?
A standard 40-hour work week over 52 weeks equals **2,080 work hours per year** (or ~2,000 hours accounting for holidays/paid time off).

#### 6. How do I convert decimal hours to minutes (e.g., 2.75 hours)?
Multiply the decimal portion by 60: $0.75 \times 60 = 45 \text{ minutes}$. Thus, $2.75 \text{ hours} = 2 \text{ hours and } 45 \text{ minutes}$.

#### 7. How many hours are in an average month?
An average calendar month (30.4375 days) contains approximately **730.5 hours**.

#### 8. What is the SI base definition of a second relative to an hour?
The SI unit of time is the second. One hour is defined as exactly **3,600 SI seconds**.
