### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### 1. What is the SI base unit for mass?
The International System of Units (SI) base unit for mass is the **kilogram (kg)**.

#### 2. How many pounds (lbs) are in 1 kilogram (kg)?
One kilogram equals approximately **2.20462 pounds** ($\approx 2.2 \text{ lbs}$).

#### 3. How many grams are in 1 pound?
One avoirdupois pound equals exactly **453.59237 grams** ($\approx 453.6 \text{ g}$).

#### 4. How many ounces are in 1 pound?
There are exactly **16 avoirdupois ounces** in 1 pound.

#### 5. How many kilograms are in 1 metric tonne?
There are exactly **1,000 kilograms** in 1 metric tonne.

#### 6. What is the difference between an Avoirdupois Ounce and a Troy Ounce?
An avoirdupois ounce equals **28.3495 grams**, whereas a Troy ounce (used for precious metals) equals **31.1035 grams**.

#### 7. What is the difference between Mass and Weight?
- **Mass:** Fundamental measure of matter inside an object ($\text{kg}$).
- **Weight:** Force exerted on mass by gravity ($\text{Weight} = m \times g$).

#### 8. How do I convert kilograms to pounds mentally?
Multiply kilograms by 2.2. For example, $70 \text{ kg} \times 2.2 = 154 \text{ pounds}$.
