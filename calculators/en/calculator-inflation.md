## Inflation Calculator – Calculate Future Purchasing Power & Real Returns

### What is Inflation?
**Inflation** is the gradual rate at which the general price level of goods and services rises in an economy, eroding consumer purchasing power over time. As inflation increases, each unit of currency buys a smaller percentage of a good or service.

An online **Inflation Calculator** estimates future purchasing power loss, projects future living expenses, and helps investors determine the **Real Rate of Return** required to preserve wealth over 5, 10, or 30-year horizons.

---

### Formula to Calculate Future Value Adjusted for Inflation

The mathematical formula used to estimate future nominal cost ($FV$) adjusted for annual inflation rate ($r$) is:

$$
FV = PV \times (1 + r)^n
$$

Where:
- **FV** = Future Value (equivalent future purchasing cost)
- **PV** = Present Value (current cost of goods or savings balance)
- **r** = Expected annual inflation rate (in decimal format, e.g., $6\% = 0.06$)
- **n** = Time horizon in years

#### Step-by-Step Numerical Example:
Suppose your current annual living expenses are **₹500,000**, and the average annual inflation rate is expected to be **6.0%** over the next **10 years**:
1. **PV** = 500,000
2. **r** = 0.06
3. **n** = 10

Plugging these parameters into the formula:

$$
FV = 500,000 \times (1 + 0.06)^{10} = 500,000 \times (1.79085) \approx ₹8,95,424
$$

In 10 years, you will need **₹895,424** to purchase the exact same basket of goods that costs **₹500,000** today.

### Real Rate of Return Formula (Fisher Effect)

To ensure your investment portfolio stays ahead of inflation, calculate your net real rate of return ($r_{real}$):

$$
r_{real} \approx r_{nominal} - i_{\text{inflation}}
$$

| Asset Class | Nominal Return | Inflation Rate | Net Real Return |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Savings Account** | 3.0% | 6.0% | **-3.0%** (Loss of purchasing power) |
| **Fixed Deposit** | 7.0% | 6.0% | **+1.0%** (Low growth) |
| **Equity Mutual Funds** | 12.0% | 6.0% | **+6.0%** (Wealth expansion) |

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. What is inflation and how does it affect savings?
Inflation erodes purchasing power. If your savings earn an interest rate lower than the inflation rate, your real wealth decreases in real purchasing terms over time.

#### 2. What is the Consumer Price Index (CPI)?
The Consumer Price Index (CPI) is an official economic indicator that measures monthly price changes in a representative basket of consumer goods and services (food, housing, transport, medical care).

#### 3. How do I protect my investments against inflation?
To beat inflation, allocate capital toward inflation-hedging asset classes such as equity mutual funds, real estate, gold, and inflation-indexed bonds.
